ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Welcome to FandomVerse

One friendly place to explore the worlds fans love.

Why we made it

Fandom information can be spread across many sites. FandomVerse brings category hubs, characters, articles, galleries, videos, events, and a small merchandise showcase together in one responsive website.

Seven communities to explore

Browse Anime, Gaming, Movies, TV Shows, K-Pop, Comics, and Manga. Each community has its own collection of stories, characters, and images. Search and filters help you find something new, and Bookmarks keep favorite items together.

Tools for fans

Read longer articles, open gallery images, watch trailers, listen to audio, check event highlights, and explore concept merchandise. The help chat can point you to pages and common features.

Built as a student project

The site is made with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and local JSON data. It adapts to smaller screens and includes a light theme. Bookmarks, theme choice, and the sample cart are kept in this browser using local storage.

What this site does not do

This front-end project has no account server or payment service. Login and sign-up screens are examples only; they do not create accounts or store form details. Merchandise is a design showcase and checkout is not connected to a seller.
